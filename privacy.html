Charon: privacy

Charon is a personal backup tool with one user, its author. It is not offered to anyone else.

It asks Google for one permission, drive.file: to create and read the files it makes itself in the author's own Google Drive. It cannot see any other file there.

Everything it stores is encrypted on the author's computer before it leaves. Charon collects nothing, shares nothing, and sends nothing anywhere but the author's own Drive.
